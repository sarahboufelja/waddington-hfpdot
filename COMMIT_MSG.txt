Add wadd_data_ingest, device instrumentation, hyperprior tests

Data ingest (src/wadd_data_ingest.py, new):
Reads GSE122662 10x HDF5 matrices and builds cell populations, with no dependency
on the embedding model -- it emits raw counts, so it is usable and testable
without the modelling stack.

- Counts are stored sparse (CSR). Single-cell expression is 90-95 percent zeros,
  so a timepoint of 7k cells by 28k genes is 78 MB sparse against 784 MB dense,
  and several lanes are concatenated per day. densify(idx) is the single explicit
  place counts become dense, so a model forward pass materialises a batch
  (57 MB) rather than a whole timepoint.
- CellAxis carries the ordered cell ids and their timepoint, and travels with
  every cell-indexed array. Arrays are permutation-invariant in their shapes, so
  require_same() compares identities rather than counts, and duplicate ids are
  rejected outright. Membership carries the labels of both of its axes for the
  same reason, and populations_from_membership therefore needs nothing else.
- Days are parsed out of filenames and cell ids numerically rather than by
  formatting the day into a search string, so D9, D9.0 and D09 all denote day 9
  and an int or float argument behave identically. available_days() enumerates
  the archive directly, replacing a dependency on an external cell_days.txt that
  is not part of the dataset yet was consulted in a dozen places.
- The cell-id prefix (day, arm, lane) is matched structurally instead of by
  counting underscores, which was correct only for one filename shape and
  silently produced a wrong prefix otherwise. The prefix is load-bearing: 10x
  barcodes are drawn from a fixed whitelist and recur across lanes (D9 C1 and C2
  share four), so without it distinct cells would collide.
- Scope is defined by the canonical filename shape plus the experiment tags,
  which excludes the GDF9 perturbation series and established DiPSC lines that
  would otherwise be admitted and then fail. A missing or non-directory
  matrix_dir is reported as a configuration fault rather than surfacing later as
  "no matrices for day N".
- Reading uses h5py rather than PyTables: it is a single wheel with HDF5 bundled,
  where PyTables pulls a large transitive tree and its wheels lag new Python and
  numpy. Declared as an optional data extra.

Device instrumentation (src/device_info.py, new):
Every experiment so far ran with JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu on a machine with two idle
GPUs, and nothing in the output said so. device_banner() reports the active
backend and warns when accelerators are present but unused; GpuMonitor samples
per-device utilisation and flags a device that stays idle during a multi-device
run. Wired into the pytest header and the E8 and E9 scripts. The mixing results
are chain properties and unaffected, but the timings are CPU timings, so 500
cells per day is a lower bound on the budget rather than a settled figure --
recorded in docs/experiments.md.

Hyperprior (tests/test_hyperprior.py):
The existing tests checked internal consistency (score equals grad, samples on
the support) but not that the target is the object it claims to be. Added tests
pinning the log-density to its definition: the generalized KL matches the
positive-measure form, the reference plan is the entropic-OT kernel exp(-C/eps),
the pi-term equals the entropy minus the transport cost over eps up to a
constant, the full density is the sum of the two marginal-KL terms and the base
term in both regimes, the constrained marginals are the row and column sums of
pi, and with the marginal terms switched off the mode is exactly the reference
plan.

Also: jnp.clip a_min/a_max keywords replaced with positional bounds. numpy 2.0
renamed those keywords and jax followed, so the old spelling silently pinned the
project to jax below 0.10. pytest moved from an optional extra to a dependency
group so a bare uv sync always installs it.

160 tests pass.
